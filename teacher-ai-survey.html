Part 1 · Digital Skills Checklist 数字技能清单

Can you… (Yes / No). Be honest — this decides what we teach. 请如实回答。

1.Turn on a smartphone and connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data
2.Type Chinese characters on your phone (pinyin input)
3.Take a clear photo of a page and share it on WhatsApp
4.Create, name and find a document or file on your phone/computer
5.Copy text from one app and paste it into another
6.Open a link and sign in to an account with a password
7.Use at least one AI chat tool (e.g., ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Gemini, Copilot)
8.Write a prompt that says: role, task, level, format, length
9.Ask the AI to change its answer (make it easier / shorter / in a table)
10.Check an AI answer against a textbook or dictionary before using it
11.Convert AI text into a printable worksheet (Word/PDF/photo)
12.Keep student names out of anything you type into an AI tool

Part 2 · AI Knowledge & Judgement(12 items, Form A)

Choose ONE answer. 单选。

1.An AI chat tool (like ChatGPT) mainly works by…
2.The AI gives you a Chinese sentence with a wrong tone mark. The best response is…
3.Which prompt will give the most useful practice set?
4."Hallucination" in AI means…
5.Before giving AI-made material to students, a teacher should ALWAYS…
6.Which information should NOT be typed into a public AI tool?
7.If the AI's exercise is too hard for your students, the quickest fix is…
8.Which is a good use of AI for exam preparation?
9.The AI writes a reading passage about "snow in Zanzibar in December". This is a problem of…
10.To make items match the CSEE format, you should…
11.A colleague says: "AI wrote it, so we don't need to check." This is…
12.The best way to know whether your AI-made practice helped students is…

Part 3 · Self-efficacy 自效能

1 = Strongly disagree · 2 = Disagree · 3 = Neutral · 4 = Agree · 5 = Strongly agree

1.I can write a clear prompt that gets the AI to produce what I need.
2.I can produce a one-page revision outline for my class with AI in under 20 minutes.
3.I can produce a 10-item practice set with an answer key with AI.
4.I can find and fix errors in AI-generated Chinese (characters, pinyin, tones, grammar).
5.I can adapt AI material to my students' level.
6.I can judge whether AI material fits the Zanzibar syllabus and exam.
7.I can use AI on my phone without help.
8.I feel confident teaching a lesson built on materials I made with AI.

Part 4 · Current Use 使用现状

1.In the last month, how many times did you use an AI tool for teaching?
2.Which device do you mainly use?
3.Which tools have you used? (tick all) 用过哪些工具
4.What have you made with AI so far? (tick all) 用 AI 做过什么
5.Biggest difficulty so far (one line). 目前最大的困难(一句话)

Part 5 · Performance Task 实操任务(done separately, 40 min)

After submitting this survey, the facilitator will start the 40-minute task: using any AI tool on your phone or computer, produce for a Form 4 class (1) a ONE-page revision outline and (2) a TEN-item practice set with answer key, on the unit Numbers, time and dates(数字、时间与日期) — HSK1–2 level; include at least one item on 几点/什么时候 and one on dates. You may edit the AI's output as much as you like.

Submit as a photo / file / pasted text on WhatsApp to SAY, with your teacher code only in the message. We score the material, not your Chinese.
